Privacy policy

Last updated: August 2026

This privacy policy explains how LEDFSWAS handles information when you visit this website or contact us about our products and services.

Information we collect

We may receive information that you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, and message when you contact us. This website does not require an account and does not intentionally collect payment information.

How we use information

We use contact information to respond to questions, provide customer support, communicate about products, and maintain the website. We do not sell personal information.

Amazon Ads API data

If you authorize an application associated with LEDFSWAS to access Amazon Ads data, that access is used only to read and analyze the advertiser's own advertising campaigns, ad groups, keywords, targets, and performance reports. We do not sell or share that data with unrelated third parties. We retain API data only for as long as needed for the requested business purpose and will delete it when no longer needed or when authorization is revoked, subject to legal or operational requirements.

Data security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect information. No online service can guarantee absolute security, so please avoid sending sensitive credentials or identity documents by email or through this website.

Cookies and analytics

This starter website does not intentionally include advertising cookies or third-party analytics scripts. If analytics or other third-party services are added later, this policy should be updated before those services are used.

Your choices

You may ask us to correct or delete information you have provided, or to explain how it is used. You may also revoke an application's Amazon Ads authorization through the relevant Amazon account settings.
